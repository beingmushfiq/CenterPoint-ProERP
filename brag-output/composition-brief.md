## Creative Direction
- Tone preset: cinematic
- Creative direction: Deadpan enterprise launch — controlled pace, big deliberate typography, wide reveals of the UI. The scale of the platform is the drama. No jokes — the spec sheet is the joke.
- Interpretation: Controlled, precise, never showy. Restraint is the personality.
- Angle: "We built an entire factory." Full-stack, multi-tenant industrial ERP + omnichannel retail platform — factory floor to POS register to courier dispatch — built with 730+ passing tests, 706 wired API routes, and 100% bilingual (English / বাংলা) coverage across 17 workspaces.
- Hook: The phrase "POS Terminal" types out on a dark charcoal screen with blinking monospace cursor, then the full POS shell slams in.
- Outro / punchline: "CenterPoint ProERP" -> "Next-Generation Industrial Manufacturing & Omnichannel Retail Operating System" -> "730+ tests passing." Dry logo hit on test count. Cut to black.
- Avoid:
  - Generic SaaS language
  - Abstract filler visuals
  - Unrelated visual redesign
