The SME Reality

The Liquidity Blindspot & The Bank Credit Gap

82% of SME failures stem from cash flow mismanagement, not lack of demand.

⚠️ The SME Internal Dilemma
  • • Operational Blindness: Vital runway and burn metrics remain trapped in manual bookkeeping spreadsheets.
  • • Receivables Drag: 30–60 day payment delays from enterprise buyers lock up working capital.
  • • Desperation Timing: SMEs only approach lenders after entering insolvency, when it is too late.
  • • Payroll Cliff: Wage commitments occur on fixed dates regardless of customer collection cycles.
🏦 The Commercial Bank Friction
  • • High Underwriting Costs: Manual due diligence exceeds $2,500/loan, making sub-$50k tickets unprofitable.
  • • Collateral Obstacle: Legacy underwriters require physical real estate mortgages that asset-light SMEs lack.
  • • Lack of Audited Statements: 90% of micro/small enterprises cannot produce audited multi-year accounts.
  • • Post-Disbursement Blindness: Banks have zero visibility into daily solvency after capital disbursement.

Security & Safety

Three-Tier Supervised Permission Architecture

Agent PROPOSES. Human CONFIRMS. Probabilistic models never commit unilateral mutations.

Tier 0: Read-Only
Autonomous Retrieval
`get_cash_position`, `list_overdue_invoices`, `get_pl_summary`

Zero database writes. Safe for automatic execution on chat invocation.
Tier 1: Advisory
Non-Binding Advice
`match_financing_options`

Ranks WeBank SME products. Writes informational assessment record with zero financial liability.
Tier 2: Supervised
Human Confirmation
`create_cash_flow_alert`

Agent execution is halted. Modal renders exact parameters. Requires named human supervisor signature.
⛔ Tier 3 (Autonomous Commitment): PERMANENTLY BLOCKED

Automatic loan contracting, fund disbursements, or banking transfers are strictly prohibited at the kernel level.

Compliance & Governance

Multi-Tenant Cryptographic Isolation & Auditability

Zero cross-tenant leakage with tamper-evident execution tracing.

Tenant Isolation Invariants
  • • JWT Claims Only: `tenant_id` is extracted strictly from cryptographically signed tokens.
  • • Global Query Scope: All Eloquent models inherit `BelongsToTenant` (`WHERE tenant_id = ?`).
  • • Integration Test T02: Verifies cross-tenant data requests trigger immediate 404 rejection.
